Precision Oncology Learning

01

Biomarker

A measurable biological characteristic that can inform tumour biology, prognosis, treatment response or another clinical feature.

02

Alteration matters

A gene-level label is not always enough. The exact mutation, fusion, amplification, expression state or resistance alteration can change interpretation.

03

Testing

Testing method depends on the biomarker and clinical question. Examples include IHC, ISH, PCR, tissue NGS and plasma/ctDNA approaches.

04

Evidence

OncoMap labels records using the evidence category present in the underlying research dataset; labels should be interpreted in clinical context.

05

Biomarker-driven trials

Trials may enrich or stratify participants by molecular characteristics. Always verify current eligibility and recruitment status on the trial record.

06

Research workflow

Biomarker → alteration → cancer context → diagnostic testing → therapy → evidence → clinical trials.

Educational/research tool: OncoMap is not a diagnostic, treatment-selection or medical-advice tool. Regulatory status, indications, testing requirements and trial recruitment can change.

OncoMap V4 — Methodology & About

Project objective

OncoMap is an independent precision-oncology research and knowledge platform designed to help users navigate relationships between biomarkers, molecular alterations, diagnostic testing, therapies, evidence and clinical-trial concepts.

Research workflow

Data methodology

The current release carries forward the structured 50-record OncoMap dataset. Each record contains biomarker identity, biomarker type, cancer associations, alteration information, testing approaches, example therapies, evidence category and clinical-trial search terminology.

Static biomarker information is separated conceptually from live trial information. ClinicalTrials.gov results are retrieved at query time and should not be treated as a permanent snapshot of recruitment status.

Data provenance

  • Static dataset: OncoMap 50-record research dataset.
  • Clinical trials: ClinicalTrials.gov API v2 and official trial records.
  • Primary-source principle: Verify regulatory, guideline, diagnostic and therapeutic claims against current authoritative sources.
  • Release: OncoMap V4 Portfolio Edition.

Limitations

OncoMap is not a comprehensive clinical knowledge base. Dataset coverage, evidence labels and therapy examples may be incomplete or become outdated. A record does not imply that a therapy is approved for every cancer or alteration shown.

Intended use

Educational, research and portfolio demonstration. It is not intended to diagnose disease, recommend treatment, determine patient eligibility or replace professional clinical judgment.

Project positioning

Independent precision-oncology research/knowledge-platform project. The platform demonstrates structured biomedical data modeling, search, relationship mapping, evidence-aware presentation and clinical-trial API integration.
